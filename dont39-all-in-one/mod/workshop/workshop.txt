description=[h2]Blood FX for Viewpoint: terrain relief fix[/h2]
description=For [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint[/url] 0.1.6+ with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811472160]3d Blood VFX for Viewpoint[/url] 1.5.1.
description=Viewpoint 0.1.6 added terrain relief (hills), on by default. Blood FX still draws its blood and severed parts at flat-ground height, so on raised ground they disappear under the terrain and on lower ground they float. This puts the spray, mist, splats, pools and severed parts back on the ground, and keeps "aim decides the cut part" lined up on slopes.
description=[list]
description=[*]Contains no code from Blood FX or Viewpoint; it adjusts Blood FX while it draws.
description=[*]Switches itself off on Blood FX versions other than 1.5.1, in case a Blood FX update handles terrain relief itself.
description=[*]With terrain relief off nothing changes.
description=[/list]
