description=[h1]Dont39 All-in-One[/h1]
description=A collection of fixes and tweaks for Build 42. Parts that work with other mods switch themselves on only when those mods are enabled, and stay idle otherwise.
description=
description=[h2]Requirements[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3619862853]ZombieBuddy[/url] 2.3.4 or newer with its Java loader installed (it asks you once to approve this mod's Java code).
description=[*]ZombieBuddyKotlin, the Kotlin runtime for ZombieBuddy mods (also by Dont39).
description=[*]Zabric, included in this item: enable it too. ZombieBuddy has to load it at game start, before the game, so that it can open up game internals the fixes read. On its first launch it registers itself with ZombieBuddy and shows a message asking you to restart the game once; from then on it loads at game start. Until then, the fixes that need it stay off and say why in console.txt.
description=[*]Project Zomboid 42.21
description=[/list]
description=Load order: ZombieBuddy, ZombieBuddyKotlin, Zabric, then the mods below that you use, then Dont39 All-in-One.
description=
description=[h2]Blood FX for Viewpoint: terrain relief fix[/h2]
description=For [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3809306528]Project Viewpoint[/url] 0.1.6+ with [url=https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3811472160]3d Blood VFX for Viewpoint[/url] 1.5.1.
description=Viewpoint 0.1.6 added terrain relief (hills), on by default. Blood FX still draws its blood and severed parts at flat-ground height, so on raised ground they disappear under the terrain and on lower ground they float. This puts the spray, mist, splats, pools and severed parts back on the ground, and keeps "aim decides the cut part" lined up on slopes.
description=[list]
description=[*]Contains no code from Blood FX or Viewpoint; it adjusts Blood FX while it draws.
description=[*]Switches itself off on Blood FX versions other than 1.5.1, in case a Blood FX update handles terrain relief itself. To use it anyway: Options > Mods > Dont39: Blood FX for Viewpoint fix > Apply to untested Blood FX versions.
description=[*]With terrain relief off nothing changes.
description=[/list]
description=Thanks to sour_kisel for Blood FX and to the Project Viewpoint team.
description=
description=[h2]Mod Options sidebar[/h2]
description=Options > Mods shows every mod's options on one long page. This adds a list of those mods on the right: click a mod to jump to its options. The list highlights the mod you are looking at while you scroll.
description=[list]
description=[*]Remembers the mod you last picked and opens the page there, also after a restart (saved in Zomboid/Lua/Dont39AllInOne_ModOptionsSidebar.txt).
description=[*]Only adds the list; option values, Apply, Accept and Back work as before.
description=[*]If another mod changes that page in a way it does not expect, the page still works, just without the list.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Keyboard controls with an IME on[/h2]
description=With an input method (IME) such as Chinese Pinyin switched on, your keys go into the IME instead of the game, so movement and hotkeys stop working. This keeps the IME out of the game window, so keys control the game whatever the IME state, and brings it back while you type in a text box (chat, search, names), where it works as before.
description=[list]
description=[*]Windows only; elsewhere it stays idle.
description=[*]The IME keeps the mode (e.g. Chinese or English) you last used in a text box.
description=[*]While you type, the IME's input (e.g. the pinyin) and its candidate list show at the text cursor; without this you type blind. Windowed mode only: in borderless or fullscreen mode the game covers the whole screen, and IMEs such as WeChat IME then hide their windows.
description=[*]Other programs' IME is not affected.
description=[/list]
